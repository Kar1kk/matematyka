Reflections in the axes and the origin

Reflection in an axis is "folding the sheet along the axis": it changes the sign of one coordinate. Reflection in $O$ is a rotation by $180^\circ$: it changes the sign of both.

IX.5

1. Formulas

reflection$(x,y)\mapsto$center of a circle $(a,b)\mapsto$
in $Ox$$(x,-y)$$(a,-b)$
in $Oy$$(-x,y)$$(-a,b)$
in $O$$(-x,-y)$$(-a,-b)$
Reflection in the axis $Ox$ changes $y$, not $x$! The point "jumps" across the axis $Ox$, so its height changes.
Image of the circle $(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$
$$S_{Ox}\colon\ (x-a)^2+(y+b)^2=r^2$$ $$S_{Oy}\colon\ (x+a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$$ $$S_{O}\colon\ (x+a)^2+(y+b)^2=r^2$$
principle
a reflection does not change distances, so the radius stays and the center goes to the image of the center

A polygon: find the images of the vertices and join them in the same order. A line / graph: in the equation replace $y\to-y$ ($Ox$), $x\to-x$ ($Oy$) or both ($O$), e.g. $y=f(x)$ goes to $y=-f(x)$, $y=f(-x)$, $y=-f(-x)$. The CKE formula sheet has the formulas for the images of a point under reflections in $Ox$, $Oy$ and a point; the replacement in the equation of a graph follows from them directly.

Composition of two reflections in axes

$(x,y)\xrightarrow{Ox}(x,-y)\xrightarrow{Oy}(-x,-y)$, so $S_{Oy}\circ S_{Ox}=S_O$. Two reflections in perpendicular axes give a rotation by $180^\circ$. That is why point reflection preserves the order of the vertices (the orientation), while reflection in an axis reverses it.

2. Interactive: reflecting a polygon

Drag the vertices $A,B,C,D$ and play the reflection. Note the order of the letters: after a reflection in an axis they go around the figure the other way, after a point reflection they do not.

3. Interactive: the image of a circle

Drag the center $S$ (blue circle) and change $r$. The images: red in $Ox$, green in $Oy$, purple in $O$.

4. Worked example

A triangle $A=(1,2)$, $B=(4,1)$, $C=(3,5)$ and the circle $(x-2)^2+(y+3)^2=4$. Find the image of the triangle under reflection in $O$ and the image of the circle under reflection in $Oy$.

  1. $S_O$: we change both signs: $A'=(-1,-2)$, $B'=(-4,-1)$, $C'=(-3,-5)$.
  2. The center of the circle $(2,-3)$ goes under $S_{Oy}$ to $(-2,-3)$. The radius $2$ is unchanged.

$A'B'C'$: $(-1,-2)$, $(-4,-1)$, $(-3,-5)$; circle $(x+2)^2+(y+3)^2=4$

5. Exercises

  1. easy
    Find the images of the point $P=(-3,5)$ under reflection in $Ox$, $Oy$ and $O$.
    Solution
    $(-3,-5)$, $(3,5)$, $(3,-5)$
  2. easy
    Find the image of the circle $(x+1)^2+(y-4)^2=9$ under reflection in the origin.
    Solution
    Center $(-1,4)\mapsto(1,-4)$, $r=3$. $(x-1)^2+(y+4)^2=9$
  3. medium
    The points $A=(2m-1,\ 3)$ and $B=(5,\ n+1)$ are symmetric about the axis $Oy$. Find $m$ and $n$.
    Solution
    In $Oy$: opposite $x$, equal $y$. $2m-1=-5$, $n+1=3$. $m=-2$, $n=2$
  4. medium
    Find the image of the line $y=2x-3$ under reflection in the axis $Ox$ and in the point $O$.
    Solution
    $Ox$: $-y=2x-3$, i.e. $y=-2x+3$. $O$: $-y=2\cdot(-x)-3$, i.e. $y=2x+3$. $y=-2x+3$ and $y=2x+3$
  5. exam-level
    $A=(2,1)$, $B=(5,4)$, and $A'$, $B'$ are their images under reflection in $Oy$. Find the area of the quadrilateral $ABB'A'$.
    Solution
    $A'=(-2,1)$, $B'=(-5,4)$. $AA'$ and $BB'$ are horizontal, so this is a trapezoid: bases $|AA'|=4$, $|BB'|=10$, height $4-1=3$. $P=\frac{4+10}{2}\cdot3$. $21$
  6. exam-level
    The circle $o\colon(x-3)^2+(y-4)^2=r^2$ and its image under reflection in $O$ are externally tangent. Find $r$ and the point of tangency.
    Solution
    The centers $S=(3,4)$, $S'=(-3,-4)$, $|SS'|=10$. External tangency: $|SS'|=r+r$, so $r=5$. The point of tangency lies at the midpoint of $SS'$, i.e. at the center of symmetry. $r=5$, point $(0,0)$

6. On the exam

  • The most common mistake: mixing up the axes. In $Ox$ the $y$ changes, in $Oy$ the $x$ changes. If in doubt, draw a single point.
  • The image of a circle: the radius is unchanged, you transform only the center.
  • The image of a line: substitute $-x$ or $-y$ into the equation and only then rearrange. Check: the image of one point must satisfy the new equation.
  • Give the vertices of the image with the labels $A'$, $B'$… in the same order.
