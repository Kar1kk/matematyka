Composition of functions

$(f\circ g)(x)=f(g(x))$ is two machines in a row: $x$ goes into $g$, and the result $g(x)$ goes into $f$. The function that stands closer to $x$, that is on the right, acts first.

V.R.2

1. Definition and domain

Definition The composition of functions $f$ and $g$ is the function $$(f\circ g)(x)=f\big(g(x)\big).$$ Its domain consists of those $x$ in the domain of $g$ for which $g(x)$ lies in the domain of $f$: $$D_{f\circ g}=\{x\in D_g:\ g(x)\in D_f\}.$$
In general $f\circ g\neq g\circ f$. For $f(x)=x+1$, $g(x)=x^2$: $f(g(x))=x^2+1$, while $g(f(x))=(x+1)^2$.

Decomposing into a composition (useful for derivatives): $h(x)=\sqrt{x^2-4}$ is $f(g(x))$ for $g(x)=x^2-4$ (“inside”) and $f(t)=\sqrt t$ (“outside”). A shift $f(x-a)$ is also a composition: $f\circ g$ with $g(x)=x-a$. There are no composition formulas on the formula sheet.

2. Interactive: the machine $x\to g\to f$

Move $x$ (the slider or the red point on the axis). The arrows: up to the graph of $g$, sideways to the line $y=x$ (this is how the value $g(x)$ becomes the new argument), vertically to the graph of $f$, and back above $x$. The red curve is $f\circ g$; the thickened segment of the $Ox$ axis is its domain.

3. $f\circ g$ or $g\circ f$?

The same two functions composed in two orders. Red: $f\circ g$, purple: $g\circ f$. Almost always these are two different graphs, often with different domains.

4. Step-by-step example

$f(x)=\sqrt x$, $g(x)=4-x^2$. Find the formulas and domains of $f\circ g$ and $g\circ f$.

  1. $(f\circ g)(x)=\sqrt{4-x^2}$. The condition $g(x)\in D_f$: $4-x^2\ge0\iff x\in[-2,2]$.
  2. $(g\circ f)(x)=4-(\sqrt x)^2$. The condition $x\in D_f$: $x\ge0$.
  3. For $x\ge0$: $(\sqrt x)^2=x$, so $(g\circ f)(x)=4-x$, but only for $x\ge0$.

$f\circ g=\sqrt{4-x^2}$, $D=[-2,2]$; $\;g\circ f=4-x$, $D=[0,\infty)$

Trap: simplifying a formula does not extend the domain. This is the demo's starting setup in section 2.

5. Tasks

  1. easy
    $f(x)=2x-3$, $g(x)=x^2$. Find $(f\circ g)(3)$ and $(g\circ f)(3)$ and give the formulas of both compositions.
    Solution
    $f(g(3))=f(9)=15$, $g(f(3))=g(3)=9$. $15$ and $9$; $(f\circ g)(x)=2x^2-3$, $(g\circ f)(x)=(2x-3)^2$
  2. medium
    $f(x)=\frac{1}{x-1}$, $g(x)=x^2-3$. Find the formula and the domain of $f\circ g$.
    Solution
    $f(g(x))=\frac{1}{x^2-3-1}=\frac{1}{x^2-4}$. The condition $g(x)\neq1\iff x^2\neq4$. $(f\circ g)(x)=\frac{1}{x^2-4}$, $D=\R\setminus\{-2,2\}$
  3. medium
    $f(x)=x^2+1$. Find a linear function $g$ such that $(f\circ g)(x)=x^2-6x+10$.
    Solution
    $x^2-6x+10=(x-3)^2+1=f(x-3)$. Since $(3-x)^2=(x-3)^2$, the other possibility works too. $g(x)=x-3$ or $g(x)=3-x$
  4. medium
    Write $h(x)=\log_2(x^2+4)$ as a composition $f\circ g$ and find the range of $h$.
    Solution
    $g(x)=x^2+4$, $f(t)=\log_2 t$. $\operatorname{range} g=[4,\infty)$, and $\log_2$ is increasing, so $h(x)\ge\log_2 4=2$. $\operatorname{range} h=[2,\infty)$
  5. exam-level
    $f(x)=\frac{x+1}{x-2}$. Find the formula and the domain of $f\circ f$, and then solve the equation $(f\circ f)(x)=1$.
    Solution
    $f(f(x))=\frac{\frac{x+1}{x-2}+1}{\frac{x+1}{x-2}-2}=\frac{2x-1}{-x+5}$. Domain: $x\neq2$ and $f(x)\neq2\iff x+1\neq2x-4\iff x\neq5$. The equation: $2x-1=5-x\iff x=2\notin D$. (Alternatively: $f(t)=1$ has no solutions, because $y=1$ is an asymptote.) $(f\circ f)(x)=\frac{2x-1}{5-x}$, $D=\R\setminus\{2,5\}$; the equation has no solutions

6. On the exam

  • Order: $f\circ g$ – $g$ first. Substitute the whole formula $g(x)$ in place of each $x$ in the formula of $f$, in brackets.
  • Find the domain from the conditions $x\in D_g$ and $g(x)\in D_f$, before you simplify the formula.
  • Check the solutions of an equation involving a composition against the domain of the composition (see task 5).
  • The range of a composition: first $\operatorname{range} g$, then the values of $f$ on that set.
