Tangent line to the graph of a function

The tangent line is the line that at the point of tangency has the same slope as the graph. Its slope is $f'(x_0)$. Every problem comes down to finding the point of tangency $x_0$.

XIII.R.3, XIII.R.4

1. Equation of the tangent line

Tangent at the point $(x_0,f(x_0))$ on CKE sheet
$$y=f'(x_0)(x-x_0)+f(x_0)$$
$f'(x_0)$
the slope, $f'(x_0)=\tan\alpha$
$x_0$
the first coordinate of the point of tangency
GivenFind $x_0$ from the equation
point of tangency $x_0$ready, substitute into the formula
tangent $\parallel y=mx+k$$f'(x_0)=m$
tangent $\perp y=mx+k$ ($m\neq0$)$f'(x_0)=-\frac1m$
tangent through $P=(p,q)$$q=f'(x_0)(p-x_0)+f(x_0)$
A point $P$ off the graph is not the point of tangency. Do not compute $f'(p)$. The unknown is $x_0$, and you substitute $P$ into the equation of the tangent.

4. Worked example

Find the equation of the tangent to the graph of $f(x)=x^3-2x$ at the point with $x$-coordinate $x_0=1$.

  1. $f(1)=1-2=-1$.
  2. $f'(x)=3x^2-2$, $\;f'(1)=1$.
  3. $y=1\cdot(x-1)+(-1)$.

$y=x-2$

Find the tangents to the graph of $f(x)=x^2$ passing through $P=(1,-3)$.

  1. The tangent at an unknown $x_0$: $y=2x_0(x-x_0)+x_0^2=2x_0x-x_0^2$.
  2. It passes through $P$: $-3=2x_0-x_0^2$, i.e. $x_0^2-2x_0-3=0$.
  3. $x_0=3$ or $x_0=-1$. Substitute into step 1: $y=6x-9$ and $y=-2x-1$.

$y=6x-9$ and $y=-2x-1$

5. Exercises

  1. easy
    Find the equation of the tangent to the graph of $f(x)=x^2-3x+1$ at the point with $x$-coordinate $2$.
    Solution
    $f(2)=-1$, $f'(x)=2x-3$, $f'(2)=1$. $y=(x-2)-1$. $y=x-3$
  2. medium
    Find the tangents to the graph of $f(x)=\frac1x$ parallel to the line $y=-4x+1$.
    Solution
    $f'(x_0)=-\frac{1}{x_0^2}=-4$, so $x_0=\pm\frac12$. The points $\left(\frac12,2\right)$ and $\left(-\frac12,-2\right)$. $y=-4x+4$ and $y=-4x-4$
  3. medium
    Find the tangents to the graph of $f(x)=x^3-3x^2+2$ parallel to the axis $Ox$.
    Solution
    $f'(x)=3x^2-6x=3x(x-2)=0$: $x_0=0$ or $x_0=2$. $f(0)=2$, $f(2)=-2$. $y=2$ and $y=-2$
  4. medium
    Find the tangent to the graph of $f(x)=\frac{x+1}{x-1}$ at the point $A=(2,3)$.
    Solution
    $$f'(x)=\frac{(x-1)-(x+1)}{(x-1)^2}=\frac{-2}{(x-1)^2}$$ $f'(2)=-2$, $y=-2(x-2)+3$. $y=-2x+7$
  5. exam-level
    Find the tangents to the graph of $f(x)=x^2+1$ passing through the origin.
    Solution
    The tangent at $x_0$: $y=2x_0(x-x_0)+x_0^2+1$. The point $(0,0)$: $0=-2x_0^2+x_0^2+1$, i.e. $x_0^2=1$. $y=2x$ and $y=-2x$
  6. exam-level
    For which $b$ is the line $y=2x+b$ tangent to the graph of $f(x)=x^3-x$?
    Solution
    Slope: $f'(x_0)=3x_0^2-1=2$, so $x_0=\pm1$. $f(1)=0$: the tangent $y=2(x-1)$, i.e. $b=-2$. $f(-1)=0$: $y=2(x+1)$, $b=2$. $b=-2$ or $b=2$

6. On the exam

  • Always start with the question: what is $x_0$? The rest is substitution into the formula from the formula sheet.
  • "A tangent through the point $P$": check whether $P$ lies on the graph. If not, you solve an equation with the unknown $x_0$ (often quadratic, sometimes with two solutions).
  • Parallel: equal slopes. Perpendicular: the product equals $-1$.
  • The angle of inclination of the tangent: $\tan\alpha=f'(x_0)$; e.g. $45^\circ$ means $f'(x_0)=1$.
  • Give the answer in the form $y=ax+b$. When there are several values of $x_0$, give all the tangents.
