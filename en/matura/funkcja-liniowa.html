Linear function

A step of $1$ to the right always changes the value by the same amount: by $a$. That is why the graph is a line, $a$ says how steeply it goes, and $b$ where it crosses the $Oy$ axis.

V.5, V.6, V.11

1. The formula and the meaning of the coefficients

Linear function
$$f(x)=ax+b$$
$a$
slope: by how much $f(x)$ changes when $x$ increases by $1$. $a>0$: increasing, $a<0$: decreasing, $a=0$: constant
$b$
constant term: $f(0)=b$, so the graph crosses $Oy$ at $(0,b)$
$x_0$
zero (root) (when $a\neq0$): $x_0=-\frac{b}{a}$
Angle of inclination and two points on CKE sheet
$$a=\tan\alpha,\qquad a=\frac{y_B-y_A}{x_B-x_A}$$
$\alpha$
the angle between the line and the positive half of the $Ox$ axis (measured upward from the axis); $a<0 \iff \alpha$ is obtuse
$A,B$
any two points of the graph, $x_A\neq x_B$
The lines $y=a_1x+b_1$ and $y=a_2x+b_2$ are parallel $\iff a_1=a_2$, and perpendicular $\iff a_1\cdot a_2=-1$. A vertical line $x=c$ is not the graph of a function.

In word problems (V.11): $a$ is the rate of change (PLN per km, liters per minute), $b$ is the starting value (a fixed fee, an initial amount).

3. The formula from two points

Drag the points $A$ and $B$. The slope is always the quotient $\Delta y/\Delta x$, no matter which two points of the line you choose.

4. Worked example

Find the formula of the linear function whose graph passes through $A=(-1,5)$ and $B=(3,-3)$. Give its zero.

  1. $a=\frac{-3-5}{3-(-1)}=\frac{-8}{4}=-2$.
  2. Substitute $A$ into $y=-2x+b$: $5=-2\cdot(-1)+b$, so $b=3$.
  3. Check with $B$: $-2\cdot3+3=-3$ ✓.
  4. $-2x+3=0\iff x=\frac32$.

$f(x)=-2x+3$, $\;x_0=1.5$

5. Tasks

  1. easy
    A line inclined to the $Ox$ axis at an angle of $60^\circ$ passes through the point $P=(\sqrt3,1)$. Find its equation.
    Solution
    $a=\tan60^\circ=\sqrt3$. $1=\sqrt3\cdot\sqrt3+b=3+b$, so $b=-2$. $y=\sqrt3x-2$
  2. easy
    Find the formula of the linear function whose graph is parallel to the line $y=-\frac12x+4$ and passes through the point $(4,1)$.
    Solution
    Parallel, so $a=-\frac12$. $1=-\frac12\cdot4+b$, so $b=3$. $f(x)=-\frac12x+3$
  3. medium
    A linear function $f$ has zero $3$ and $f(2)=f(-1)+6$. Find its formula.
    Solution
    $f(2)-f(-1)=a\cdot(2-(-1))=3a=6$, so $a=2$. From $f(3)=0$: $6+b=0$, $b=-6$. $f(x)=2x-6$
  4. medium
    For which $m$ is the function $f(x)=(2m-6)x+m+1$ decreasing and its graph crosses the $Oy$ axis above the $Ox$ axis?
    Solution
    Decreasing: $2m-6<0\iff m<3$. The crossing with $Oy$ is at $(0,m+1)$, so $m+1>0\iff m>-1$. $m\in(-1,3)$
  5. exam-level
    The price of a taxi ride is a fixed fee plus a rate for each kilometer. For $4$ km someone paid $22$ PLN, and for $10$ km $40$ PLN. How much does a $15$ km ride cost? How many kilometers can you ride for $70$ PLN?
    Solution
    $k(x)=ax+b$. $a=\frac{40-22}{10-4}=3$ PLN/km, $b=22-3\cdot4=10$ PLN. $k(15)=55$, and $3x+10=70\iff x=20$. $55$ PLN; $20$ km

6. On the exam

  • Always check the formula with the second point. It takes 10 seconds, and a sign error in $a$ is the most common slip.
  • $\tan\alpha=a$ refers to the angle with the positive half of the $Ox$ axis. For $a<0$ the angle is obtuse, e.g. $a=-1\Rightarrow\alpha=135^\circ$.
  • In problems with a parameter write the conditions separately ($a<0$, $b>0$) and take the intersection.
  • Word problem: name the variables with units and state the domain (e.g. $x\ge0$ km).
