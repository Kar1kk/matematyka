Cheat sheet: formulas not on the CKE sheet

These are not on the CKE formula sheet (“Wybrane wzory matematyczne”, 2023 edition), yet they come in handy on the exam. Memorise them or be able to derive them quickly. The link next to each item leads to the topic.

1. Algebra and numbers

Sum of powers, odd n · topic
$$a^n+b^n=(a+b)\,\cdot$$ $$(a^{n-1}-a^{n-2}b+\ldots+b^{n-1})$$
note
only for odd $n$ (substitute $-b$ for $b$ in the formula for $a^n-b^n$, which is on the sheet)
Bézout's theorem · topic
$$W(a)=0\iff (x-a)\mid W(x)$$
remainder
of the division by $x-a$ equals $W(a)$
Viète: symmetric expressions · topic
$$x_1^2+x_2^2=s^2-2p$$ $$x_1^3+x_2^3=s^3-3ps$$
$s,\,p$
$s=x_1+x_2$, $p=x_1x_2$
Logarithm, a power in the base · topic
$$\log_{a^k}b=\tfrac1k\log_a b$$

2. Percentages

Compounding more often than annually · topic
$$K=K_0\left(1+\tfrac{p}{100m}\right)^{mn}$$
Loan instalment (derive it) · topic
$$R=\frac{Kq^n(q-1)}{q^n-1}$$
$q$
$1+\frac{r}{100}$, $r$ – interest rate per period
