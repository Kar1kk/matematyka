Absolute value

$|x-a|$ is simply the distance between points $x$ and $a$ on the number line. Almost every problem with an absolute value is solved faster once you read it as a statement about distance.

I.7 (inequalities: III.R.4)

1. Definition and properties

Definition on CKE sheet
$$|x|=\begin{cases} x, & x\ge0\\ -x, & x<0\end{cases}$$
$|x|$
the distance of $x$ from $0$; it “removes the minus”, e.g. $|-7|=7$
$|x-a|$
the distance of $x$ from $a$. Note: $|x+4|=|x-(-4)|$ is the distance from $-4$
$\sqrt{x^2}$
$=|x|$ on CKE sheet
Properties on CKE sheet
$$|x|\ge0,\qquad |-x|=|x|$$ $$|xy|=|x||y|,\qquad \left|\frac xy\right|=\frac{|x|}{|y|}$$ $$|x+y|\le|x|+|y|$$
triangle
the equality $|x+y|=|x|+|y|$ holds only when $xy\ge0$ (the same signs)
Where does the triangle inequality come from?

By definition $-|x|\le x\le|x|$ and $-|y|\le y\le|y|$. Add side by side:

$$-(|x|+|y|)\le x+y\le |x|+|y|,$$

which says exactly that $|x+y|\le|x|+|y|$ (the number lies no farther from zero than $|x|+|y|$).

4. Step-by-step example

Solve the equation $|x+4|=5$.

  1. Geometrically: we look for points at distance $5$ from $-4$: $-4-5=-9$ and $-4+5=1$.
  2. Algebraically: $x+4=5$ or $x+4=-5$, that is $x=1$ or $x=-9$.

$x\in\{-9,\,1\}$

Evaluate $|\sqrt3-2|+|\sqrt3-1|$.

  1. $\sqrt3\approx1.73$: $\sqrt3-2<0$, so $|\sqrt3-2|=2-\sqrt3$.
  2. $\sqrt3-1>0$, so $|\sqrt3-1|=\sqrt3-1$.

$2-\sqrt3+\sqrt3-1=1$

5. Tasks

  1. easy
    Evaluate $|-3|-|2-7|+|\pi-4|$.
    Solution
    $\pi-4<0$, so $|\pi-4|=4-\pi$. $3-5+4-\pi=$ $2-\pi$
  2. easy
    Solve the equations: a) $|x-2|=3$, b) $|2x+1|=5$.
    Solution
    a) points at distance $3$ from $2$: $x=-1$ or $x=5$. b) $2x+1=5$ or $2x+1=-5$: $x=2$ or $x=-3$.
  3. medium
    Evaluate $\sqrt{(1-\sqrt2)^2}+|\sqrt2-3|$.
    Solution
    $\sqrt{(1-\sqrt2)^2}=|1-\sqrt2|=\sqrt2-1$, $\;|\sqrt2-3|=3-\sqrt2$. The sum: $2$
  4. medium
    How many integers $x$ satisfy the inequality $|x-3|<4$?
    Solution
    Distance from $3$ less than $4$: $x\in(-1,7)$. The integers $0,1,\dots,6$. $7$ numbers
  5. medium
    It is known that $|a|=3$ and $|b|=5$. What values can $|a+b|$ take?
    Solution
    $a=\pm3$, $b=\pm5$. Same signs: $|a+b|=8$; different signs: $|a+b|=2$. $2$ or $8$ (consistent with $|a+b|\le|a|+|b|=8$)
  6. exam-level
    Prove that if $|x-1|<2$ and $|y+1|<3$, then $|x+y|<5$.
    Solution
    $x+y=(x-1)+(y+1)$. By the triangle inequality $|x+y|\le|x-1|+|y+1|<2+3=5$. $|x+y|<5$, which completes the proof.

6. On the exam

  • The sign inside: $|x+4|$ is the distance from $-4$, not from $4$.
  • $\sqrt{x^2}=|x|$: before dropping the absolute value, check the sign of the expression (e.g. $|\pi-4|=4-\pi$).
  • $|\,\cdot\,|=r$ with $r<0$: no solutions — say so in one sentence, without calculations.
  • Two absolute values, or an absolute value with an expression on the right-hand side: the interval method and checking whether the solution belongs to the interval.
