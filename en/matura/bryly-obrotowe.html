Cylinder, cone, sphere

Solids of revolution are formed by rotating a plane figure about an axis: a rectangle, a right triangle, a semicircle. Their axial cross-section is that figure together with its reflection, and in it you compute almost everything.

X.4, X.5

1. Formulas on CKE sheet

Cylinder
$$V=\pi r^2h,\qquad P_b=2\pi rh$$ $$P_c=2\pi r(r+h)$$
axial cross-section
a rectangle $2r\times h$
Cone
$$V=\frac13\pi r^2h,\qquad P_b=\pi rl$$ $$P_c=\pi r(r+l),\quad l^2=r^2+h^2$$
axial cross-section
an isosceles triangle: base $2r$, legs $l$ (slant height)
Sphere
$$V=\frac43\pi r^3,\qquad P=4\pi r^2$$
axial cross-section
a great circle of radius $r$; every plane section is a circle
Angles in a cone The apex angle $2\alpha$ is the angle between opposite slant lines (the angle at the apex of the axial cross-section): $\sin\alpha=\frac rl$. The angle between the slant line and the base $\beta$: $\cos\beta=\frac rl$, $\tan\beta=\frac hr$, and $\alpha+\beta=90^\circ$.
The lateral surface of a cone, when unrolled, is a circular sector of radius $l$ and arc length $2\pi r$; its central angle is $\frac{r}{l}\cdot360^\circ$ (worth remembering).

3. Step-by-step example

The axial cross-section of a cone is an equilateral triangle of side $6$. Find the volume, the total surface area and the apex angle of the cone.

  1. The side of the triangle is the slant height and the diameter: $l=6$, $r=3$. The height of the equilateral triangle: $h=\frac{6\sqrt3}{2}=3\sqrt3$.
  2. $V=\frac13\pi\cdot9\cdot3\sqrt3=9\sqrt3\pi$, $\;P_c=\pi\cdot3\cdot(3+6)=27\pi$.
  3. The angle at the apex of an equilateral triangle: $60^\circ$.

$V=9\sqrt3\pi$, $P_c=27\pi$, apex angle $60^\circ$

4. Tasks

  1. easy
    A cylinder has base radius $3$ and height $5$. Find $V$ and $P_c$.
    Solution
    $V=\pi\cdot9\cdot5$, $P_c=2\pi\cdot3\cdot(3+5)$. $V=45\pi$, $P_c=48\pi$
  2. easy
    A sphere has volume $36\pi$. Find its surface area.
    Solution
    $\frac43\pi r^3=36\pi\Rightarrow r^3=27$, $r=3$. $P=4\pi\cdot9$. $P=36\pi$
  3. medium
    The slant height of a cone is $10$ and it is inclined to the base at an angle of $60^\circ$. Find $V$ and $P_b$.
    Solution
    $r=10\cos60^\circ=5$, $h=10\sin60^\circ=5\sqrt3$. $V=\frac13\pi\cdot25\cdot5\sqrt3$, $P_b=\pi\cdot5\cdot10$. $V=\frac{125\sqrt3}{3}\pi$, $P_b=50\pi$
  4. medium
    The lateral surface of a cone, when unrolled, is a semicircle of radius $8$. Find the apex angle and the volume of the cone.
    Solution
    $l=8$; the arc of the semicircle $8\pi=2\pi r$, so $r=4$. $\sin\alpha=\frac48=\frac12$, $\alpha=30^\circ$. $h=\sqrt{64-16}=4\sqrt3$, $V=\frac13\pi\cdot16\cdot4\sqrt3$. Apex angle $60^\circ$, $V=\frac{64\sqrt3}{3}\pi$
  5. exam-level
    A right triangle with legs $3$ and $4$ rotates about its hypotenuse. Find the volume and the surface area of the resulting solid.
    Solution
    Two cones with a common base are formed. Its radius is the altitude to the hypotenuse: $r=\frac{3\cdot4}{5}=\frac{12}{5}$; the sum of the heights of the cones $=5$. $V=\frac13\pi r^2\cdot5=\frac13\pi\cdot\frac{144}{25}\cdot5$. Area: slant heights $3$ and $4$, $P=\pi r\cdot3+\pi r\cdot4=\frac{84}{5}\pi$. $V=\frac{48}{5}\pi$, $P=\frac{84}{5}\pi$
  6. exam-level
    A cylinder is circumscribed about a sphere (the sphere touches both bases and the lateral surface). Find the ratio of the volume of the cylinder to the volume of the sphere and the ratio of their surface areas.
    Solution
    Cylinder: radius $r$, $h=2r$. $V_w=2\pi r^3$, $V_k=\frac43\pi r^3$; $P_w=2\pi r(r+2r)=6\pi r^2$, $P_k=4\pi r^2$. Both ratios equal $\frac32$ (a discovery of Archimedes).

5. On the exam

  • Draw the axial cross-section and mark $r$, $h$, $l$ and the angle in it. Most tasks are ordinary plane geometry in that cross-section.
  • The apex angle is $2\alpha$, not $\alpha$. Read carefully which angle is given.
  • Leave results with $\pi$ (e.g. $27\pi$), unless the text asks for an approximation.
  • Compute $P_b$ of a cone with the slant height $l$, not with the height $h$.
