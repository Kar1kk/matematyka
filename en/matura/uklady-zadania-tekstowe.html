Word problems with systems of equations

Two unknowns, two independent facts from the text, two equations. Each equation is a line, and the answer to the problem is their intersection point, as long as it makes sense in the real world.

IV.2

1. The template (always the same)

  1. Name the unknowns in words and with units: “$x$ — the price of a notebook in PLN”. Add the conditions ($x>0$, digit $1\le a\le9$).
  2. Write two equations, each from a different sentence of the text.
  3. Solve the system (substitution or opposite coefficients).
  4. Check against the text, not against the equations: is the result positive, an integer, does it fit the sentences of the problem.
  5. Answer in a full sentence, to the question that was asked.

2. Typical problems

In mixture problems write separate equations for the mass of the solution and the mass of the substance. Concentrations must not be added.

You want $M$ kg of a $p\%$ solution from a $p_1\%$ solution ($x$ kg) and a $p_2\%$ solution ($y$ kg). Blue line: the mass of the solution, purple: the mass of the substance. Set $p$ outside the interval $[p_1,p_2]$: the system has a solution, but the problem does not.

5. Step-by-step example

Cities A and B are $330$ km apart. A train leaves A at $60$ km/h, and half an hour later a train leaves B toward it at $90$ km/h. How far from A will they meet?

  1. $x$ — the distance traveled by the train from A until the meeting, $y$ — the distance traveled by the train from B (km), $x,y>0$.
  2. Together they cover the whole route: $x+y=330$. The train from A travels $0.5$ h longer: $\frac{x}{60}=\frac{y}{90}+\frac12$.
  3. Second equation times $180$: $3x=2y+90$. Substitute $y=330-x$: $5x=750$, $x=150$, $y=180$.
  4. Check against the text: A travels $2.5$ h, B travels $2$ h, so it left $0.5$ h later ✓.

The trains will meet $150$ km from A (the demo's starting settings from section 3).

6. Tasks

  1. easy
    For $3$ notebooks and $2$ pens one pays $19$ PLN, and for $2$ notebooks and $5$ pens $20$ PLN. How much does a notebook cost, and how much a pen?
    Solution
    $\begin{cases} 3x+2y=19\\ 2x+5y=20 \end{cases}$. Multiply the first by $2$, the second by $3$ and subtract: $11y=22$, $y=2$, $x=5$. A notebook costs $5$ PLN, a pen $2$ PLN.
  2. medium
    The sum of the digits of a two-digit number is $11$. After swapping the digits we get a number greater by $27$. Find this number.
    Solution
    $\overline{ab}$: $a+b=11$ and $10b+a-(10a+b)=27$, that is $b-a=3$. Hence $b=7$, $a=4$. Check: $74-47=27$ ✓. $47$
  3. medium
    A mother is $4$ times as old as her daughter. In $6$ years she will be $3$ times as old as her. How old is each of them?
    Solution
    $\begin{cases} m=4c\\ m+6=3(c+6) \end{cases}$. Substitute: $4c+6=3c+18$, $c=12$, $m=48$. In $6$ years: $54=3\cdot18$ ✓. The daughter is $12$, the mother $48$.
  4. medium
    How many kilograms of a $10\%$ solution and how many of a $40\%$ solution must be mixed to obtain $30$ kg of a $20\%$ solution?
    Solution
    Mass of the solution: $x+y=30$. Mass of the substance: $0.1x+0.4y=0.2\cdot30=6$. Times $10$: $x+4y=60$, subtract the first: $3y=30$. $20$ kg of the $10\%$ solution and $10$ kg of the $40\%$ solution (the demo's starting settings from section 4).
  5. exam-level
    A motorboat travels $36$ km downstream in $2$ h, and the same route upstream in $3$ h. Find the speed of the motorboat in still water and the speed of the current.
    Solution
    $v$ — the speed of the motorboat, $u$ — of the current (km/h): $2(v+u)=36$, $3(v-u)=36$, that is $v+u=18$, $v-u=12$. Add: $2v=30$. $v=15$ km/h, $u=3$ km/h
  6. exam-level
    Two goods cost $250$ PLN together. After the price of the first is reduced by $20\%$ and the price of the second is raised by $10\%$, they cost $236$ PLN together. What were the prices before the change?
    Solution
    $\begin{cases} x+y=250\\ 0.8x+1.1y=236 \end{cases}$. Substitute $y=250-x$: $0.8x+275-1.1x=236$, $0.3x=39$, $x=130$, $y=120$. Check: $104+132=236$ ✓. $130$ PLN and $120$ PLN

7. On the exam

  • Defining the unknowns (what they mean, in what units) is part of the solution. Without it a point for “setting up the system” is hard to get.
  • Units: convert minutes to hours ($20$ min $=\frac13$ h) before substituting into $s=vt$.
  • Percentages: $20\%$ of what? A $20\%$ discount followed by a $20\%$ increase does not return to the original price.
  • Check that it makes sense: a negative mass, a non-integer number of people, a digit $10$ mean a mistake or that the problem has no solution.
  • Answer the question in the text (e.g. “how far from B” is $y$, not $x$).
