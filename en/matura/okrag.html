Equation of a circle

A circle is the set of all points at distance exactly $r$ from the center $S$. The equation of a circle is simply the distance formula $|SP|=r$, squared.

IX.4

1. The equation of a circle

A circle with center $S=(a,b)$ and radius $r>0$
$$(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2=r^2$$
origin
$|SP|=r\iff\sqrt{(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2}=r$, square both sides. It is on the formula sheet.
disk
$(x-a)^2+(y-b)^2\le r^2$ (circle + interior)
The signs in the brackets are "reversed": $(x+3)^2+(y-1)^2=5$ means $S=(-3,1)$, $r=\sqrt5$ (not $5$!). When the right-hand side is $0$, it is just the point $S$; when it is negative, the set is empty.

The expanded form $x^2+y^2-2ax-2by+c=0$ is outside the requirements of 2024, but it is on the CKE formula sheet (with the condition $a^2+b^2-c>0$, where the center is $(a,b)$), so you may use it (if it appears: complete the square).

5. Step-by-step example

Find the equation of the circle with diameter $AB$, where $A=(-1,2)$, $B=(5,-6)$. Does the point $P=(5,2)$ lie on this circle?

  1. Center: $S=\left(\frac{-1+5}{2},\frac{2-6}{2}\right)=(2,-2)$.
  2. $|AB|=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=10$, so $r=5$.
  3. Equation: $(x-2)^2+(y+2)^2=25$.
  4. $P$: $(5-2)^2+(2+2)^2=9+16=25$ ✓.

$(x-2)^2+(y+2)^2=25$, the point $P$ lies on the circle

6. Tasks

  1. easy
    State the center and the radius of the circle $(x+4)^2+(y-3)^2=12$.
    Solution
    $a=-4$, $b=3$, $r=\sqrt{12}=2\sqrt3$. $S=(-4,3)$, $r=2\sqrt3$
  2. easy
    Find the equation of the circle with center $S=(3,-1)$ passing through $A=(-1,2)$.
    Solution
    $r^2=|SA|^2=(-4)^2+3^2=25$. $(x-3)^2+(y+1)^2=25$
  3. medium
    The circle: $(x-2)^2+(y+1)^2=20$. Determine the positions of the points $P=(1,1)$, $Q=(6,3)$, $R=(6,1)$.
    Solution
    $P$: $1+4=5<20$; $Q$: $16+16=32>20$; $R$: $16+4=20$. $P$ inside, $Q$ outside, $R$ on the circle
  4. medium
    Find the equation of the circle with center $S=(-3,4)$ tangent to the $Ox$ axis, and then the one tangent to the $Oy$ axis.
    Solution
    The distance of $S$ from $Ox$ is $|4|=4$, from $Oy$ it is $|-3|=3$. $(x+3)^2+(y-4)^2=16$ and $(x+3)^2+(y-4)^2=9$
  5. exam-level
    The center of a circle lies on the line $y=x+1$, and the circle passes through $A=(1,0)$ and $B=(5,2)$. Find its equation.
    Solution
    $S=(t,t+1)$ and $|SA|^2=|SB|^2$: $(t-1)^2+(t+1)^2=(t-5)^2+(t-1)^2$, i.e. $(t+1)^2=(t-5)^2$, $2t+1=-10t+25$, $t=2$. $S=(2,3)$, $r^2=1+9=10$; check $B$: $9+1=10$ ✓. $(x-2)^2+(y-3)^2=10$
  6. exam-level
    For which $a$ does the point $P=(1,-2)$ lie on the circle $(x-a)^2+(y-2)^2=25$?
    Solution
    $(1-a)^2+16=25$, $(1-a)^2=9$, $1-a=\pm3$. $a=-2$ or $a=4$

7. On the exam

  • The most common mistake: $r$ instead of $r^2$. On the right-hand side stands the square of the radius: $=12$ means $r=2\sqrt3$.
  • Signs: $(x+3)^2$ means $a=-3$. Write $S$ explicitly before you continue.
  • "The center lies on a line" → write $S$ with one parameter, e.g. $S=(t,t+1)$, and set up one equation.
  • Distinguish a circle (an equation, "$=$") from a disk (an inequality, "$\le$").
