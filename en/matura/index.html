Matura, extended level

The matura is the Polish school-leaving exam. Sections follow the 2024 core curriculum, which applies to the exam from 2025. Within each section, topics are in learning order: basics first, then the extended level.

Beyond the curriculum

Cheat sheet: formulas not on the CKE sheet

Formulas and facts that are not in the official “Wybrane wzory matematyczne” sheet but you need to remember.

Removed from the core curriculum

What was cut in 2024 (e.g. distance from a point to a line, standard deviation, trigonometric inequalities). Not required on the exam, but useful for olympiads and older problem books.

Theorems with proofs

The curriculum lists them explicitly. You need to know their proofs, not just the statements.
